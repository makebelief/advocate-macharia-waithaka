LEGAL GUIDE

What to Do When a Debt Dispute Arises

Begin by locating the agreement or invoices and building a dated record of payments, notices and correspondence.

Different legal mechanisms may apply depending on whether the issue involves a personal debt, secured lending, a company, or insolvency.

Before taking steps such as filing proceedings or issuing demands, obtain advice on the available remedies, limitation periods and realistic recovery options.

Note: This is general information and not legal advice for a specific case.

Arbitration or Litigation: Choosing a Dispute Path

Contracts sometimes include arbitration or mediation clauses. The wording of these clauses can affect the next steps.

Negotiation and mediation may help parties agree on a practical settlement. Arbitration can provide a private dispute-resolution process, while litigation proceeds through the courts.

The nature of the disagreement, urgency, evidence, enforceability and applicable procedure should guide the choice.

Note: This is general information and not legal advice for a specific case.

Employment Contracts: Key Things to Check

Review job duties, remuneration, probation, notice periods, leave and disciplinary procedures before signing an employment contract.

Keep written records of relevant workplace communications, policies and decisions if a dispute arises.

Kenyan employment law imposes duties and protections on both employers and employees. Seek advice suited to your specific facts.

Note: This is general information and not legal advice for a specific case.
